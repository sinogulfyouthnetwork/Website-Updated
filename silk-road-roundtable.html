The flagship table for China–Gulf dialogue.

Curated, youth-led conversations across business, policy, culture, research, technology, and youth leadership — bringing members, experts, and institutional guests to one table.

Propose a roundtable Apply to speak
The format

Small table. Serious conversation. Two regions.

Each roundtable gathers 15–40 participants around one China–Gulf question — moderated, off-the-record where needed, and always with youth voices at the center. Sessions run in English, Mandarin, or Arabic depending on the room.

  • Who's at the table: students, young professionals, researchers, diplomats, founders, and invited experts from both regions.
  • Where: our active cities — and at partner institutions, from universities to the UAE Mission to the UN.
  • Cadence: convened around moments that matter — summits, national days, market shifts, cultural seasons.
