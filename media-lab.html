Tradition in Bloom

While walking through a park, Mariam discovers a group of locals dancing and practicing kung fu. What follows is a beautiful and spontaneous exchange — where strangers became teachers, warmly inviting her to join and experience their traditions firsthand. This video captures that meaningful moment of cultural connection and shared joy.

Mariam Al Mutawa is an Emirati student currently studying in Shanghai with a deep interest in exploring cultures beyond her own.

What is the Studio?

The Media Studio is SGYN's home for original creative work about China and the Gulf. Through film, essays, photography, audio, and art, young people share the places, questions, and experiences that shape how they see both regions — and the connections between them.

The Studio is still taking shape. Each contribution helps define what it can become.

What the Studio publishes

Films Documentary moments, video essays, and visual stories — like Tradition in Bloom above.
Essays & reflections Writing on culture, identity, language, and life between two regions — in English, 中文, or العربية.
Photo essays Photography that carries a story — an event, a street, a festival, a journey.
Audio Conversations, soundscapes, and spoken-word from either shore.
Art Calligraphy, illustration, design — anything made by hand or screen that bridges the two worlds.
