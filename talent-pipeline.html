Build your China–Gulf career.

The Talent Pipeline is SGYN's professional development and opportunity-matching initiative — connecting students and early-career youth with mentorship and real-world experience in organisations operating between China and the Gulf.

Apply to the Pipeline Partner as an employer

Made for the corridor's next operators.

Whether you're writing a thesis on Gulf sovereign funds or building a startup between Shenzhen and Riyadh, the Pipeline matches your ambition to mentors, employers, and real projects.

  • Students & young professionals aiming at cross-regional careers.
  • Researchers & analysts covering China–Gulf markets and policy.
  • Founders building across both regions.
  • Policy & diplomacy-oriented youth heading for institutions.
  • Business, investment, tech, culture & media talent of every stripe.
